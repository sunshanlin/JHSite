## Priority Issues

### [P0] The first mobile screen contains no call to action and no Thai privacy notice
#cookie-bar renders 268px tall (32% of the 390x844 viewport) in English only, covering .hero-actions entirely and burying .chat-fab (LINE) at z-index 90 vs the bar's 100. The Thai strings EXIST in I18N for .cookie-title, #cookie-bar p, #cookie-accept, #cookie-decline, but applyLang() runs only on a .lang button click or when localStorage.site_lang === 'en' (line 3832). It never runs on a default Thai load, so the Thai translations are unreachable.
Why: PRODUCT.md states Thai buyers arrive via LINE on mobile. Their entire first screen is a nav, a broken headline, a clipped paragraph, and an English legal dialog — zero conversion affordances. English-only PDPA notice to a Thai audience is also a compliance smell.
Fix: line 3832 -> applyLang(localStorage.getItem('site_lang') || 'th'); collapse .cookie-bar on mobile to a single ~72px row (grid-template-columns: 1fr auto, one sentence + detail link); raise .chat-fab to z-index 110.
Suggested command: /impeccable adapt, then /impeccable clarify

### [P1] The Thai H1 renders as "Business Centralพร้อม" with no space
Markup is Dynamics 365 Business Central<br>พร้อม <span class="hero-accent">Thai Localization</span>. The <br> supplies the ONLY separation. At <=560px line 1388 (header h1 br { display:none }) removes it and line 1387 adds overflow-wrap:anywhere. Verified rendered and in the accessibility tree. English after toggling: "Business Central implementationwith complete Thai Localization". overflow-wrap:anywhere additionally licenses breaks inside Thai words at arbitrary code points.
Why: the most-read string on the page, on the dominant device, looks like a defect — for a consultant whose pitch is getting Thai details right.
Fix: real space in markup (Business Central<br> พร้อม); replace overflow-wrap:anywhere with normal plus two hand-placed <wbr> at safe Thai break points, consistent with #pricing.
Suggested command: /impeccable typeset

### [P1] The VAT/licence exclusion sits 1,309px from the price it qualifies, and its marker is hidden from screen readers
.plan-price-line strong sup carries aria-hidden="true". .pricing-excludes renders at .72rem = 11.52px (smallest text on the page) after all three cards. Measured at 390px: 1,309px between 280,000 and its footnote. PRODUCT.md: "ข้อความกำกับนี้ต้องอยู่คู่กับตัวเลขเสมอ ห้ามตัดทิ้งเพื่อความสวยงาม."
Why: the exact question a CFO asks is "is 280,000 the whole number?" The answer is 2.5 screens away in the smallest type on the site, and screen-reader users never hear the asterisk.
Fix: one-line exclusion inside each .plan-price-box beneath the figure ("ไม่รวม VAT 7% · Microsoft License · ค่าดูแลรายปี" at .8rem), keeping .pricing-excludes as the full statement. Remove aria-hidden from the <sup> and give it visually-hidden text.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

### [P1] The only two conversion buttons are English-only, and the copy that de-risks them is collapsed
.cal-actions .cta render "Phone Call / 15 min · quick questions" and "Book a Meeting (Zoom) / 1 hour · demo and scope" — verified unchanged in both TH and EN; no I18N entry exists for either. They are <button> elements with no href fallback. Reading order puts the secondary ghost button above the burgundy primary, so the higher-commitment ask is emphasised. .plan-assurance appears on only 2 of 3 pricing cards, both inside default-closed <details>, and is absent from the 280,000 card entirely.
Why: every CTA on the page funnels to #contact, and the funnel terminates in two English buttons. LINE — the channel PRODUCT.md says Thai buyers actually use — appears only in .contact-info above and in a 56px FAB invisible on first mobile load.
Fix: add both buttons to I18N with Thai default ("โทรคุย 15 นาที · ถามสั้น ๆ" / "นัด Demo ผ่าน Zoom 1 ชั่วโมง · ดูระบบและสรุปขอบเขต"); make the 15-minute call visually primary; add a LINE row inside .cal-actions; move .plan-assurance out of <details> and add one to .plan-loc.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify, then /impeccable onboard

### [P2] Mobile/a11y cluster — 32 sub-44px tap targets, 8 sub-AA contrast pairs, every anchor lands 11px short
Both assessments converge here.
Tap targets at 390px: 32 of 107 below 44x44. button.slider-play is 12x34 — the pause control that makes the auto-advancing carousel WCAG 2.2.2-compliant is itself below SC 2.5.8's 24x24 minimum and unhittable one-handed. Also lang TH/EN 33x33, menu-toggle 34x34, .feature-link x6 at 119x18, email 191x27.
Contrast: 8 real AA failures concentrated on one token pair — #5C736E on #EFEFE9 = 4.40:1 — which carries header p.lead (the hero subheadline), .tagline, and .hero-learn-link simultaneously. Plus footer span 4.32, .ci-role 3.99, .section-kicker on dark 4.25.
Anchors: .topnav measures 68.8px but the JS handler at line 3327 overrides scroll-padding-top:76px with target.offsetTop - 58. Every one of ~14 in-page anchors lands ~11px too high, and because .topnav is rgba(255,255,255,.94) the clipped text ghosts through the bar — worse for Thai than Latin because tone marks and upper vowels occupy exactly the clipped band.
Fix: .slider-play { width:44px; height:44px }; .feature-link { display:inline-flex; min-height:44px; align-items:center }; darken --ui-muted from #5C736E to ~#4E625D (~5.2:1); change -58 to -76, or delete the scrollPageTo handler entirely and let scroll-behavior:smooth + scroll-padding-top do the job (also removes ~40 lines of JS).
Suggested command: /impeccable audit, then /impeccable adapt

## Minor Observations

- .hero-proof is styled in 7 CSS rules and does not exist in the markup. A hero proof strip was designed and removed; the hero now carries zero proof elements on a page whose first principle is evidence over promises.
- Dead CSS: .contact-form input/textarea/label/.cf-note/.hp (honeypot) plus the I18N entry for .contact-note — a removed contact form left ~30 lines behind.
- .hero-actions .cta.secondary is display:none yet still in the DOM with a maintained translation.
- .hero-accent sets background-clip:text but never sets -webkit-text-fill-color:transparent (computed = rgb(166,58,86), same as color). The gradient never renders; this is the source of both gradient-text detector findings.
- No @media (forced-colors: active) anywhere. .bento-item[aria-pressed="true"] draws its dark background from a z-index:-1 ::before, so in Windows High Contrast the white h3 will sit on a white tile.
- Feature tab labels are English (VAT / WHT / Billing / Stock & Assets / Thai Documents / API) while panel headings are Thai — the visitor must hold an EN->TH mapping to navigate their own language.
- .plan-banner on both .plan-dark cards uses burgundy — non-interactive category labels wearing the color the code comment itself reserves for "action สำคัญ". Burgundy currently appears on 10 surfaces.
- Line 1521: #services > .wrap > .sub gets white-space:nowrap at >=1101px; between 1101-1250px that Thai sentence clips against body overflow-x:hidden.
- #pricing at 320px: .plan-price-line for On-Demand overflows by 10px, clipping "/ Manday".
- 34 of 47 images lack explicit width/height (CLS); 5 below-fold images lack loading="lazy".
- Cal("on", ...) registers at parse time before consent, but the generate_lead callback correctly guards on typeof window.gtag === 'function'. Safe, and well written.
- #faq has no nav entry and sits after #contact — objection handling arrives after the ask.
- Thai FAQ #1 is materially weaker than its English twin. EN states the problem ("Standard Business Central does not cover Thai tax directly, so we built JWIC Localization to fill the gap"); TH buries it.
